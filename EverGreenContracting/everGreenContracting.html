About us

Ever Green Contracting, LLC was established in early 2024 to create a customer-first remodeling and construction service.

Business partners Don Milford and Micah Toll, leveraging their remodeling experience, wanted to provide a solution-oriented approach for our customers. We offer preliminary design services in conjunction with our estimates to help you create your perfect new space.

Our focus on attention to detail creates a finished product of superior quality and craftsmanship within your budget. We work around your schedule and needs to limit the project's impact on your home, such as minimizing downtime of your kitchen or bathroom within the overall project schedule. We seldom use subcontractors so we have ultimate control of the finished product.
