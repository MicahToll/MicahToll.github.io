About us

Ever Green Contracting, LLC was established in early 2024 to create a customer first remodeling and construction service.

Business partners Don Milford and Micah Toll, leveraging their remodeling experience, wanted to provide a solution oriented approach for our customers. We can offer preliminary design services in conjunction with our estimates to help you create the perfect new space.

We focus on attention to detail and your budget to create a finished product of superior quality and craftsmanship. We work around your schedule and needs, such as minimizing downtime of your kitchen or bathroom within the overall project schedule. We seldom use subcontractors so we have ultimate control of the finished product.

To have a discussion about your space upgrade, contact us.
